* **Networking** = crucial for **pod & cluster resource comm**.
* Each **Pod** has **unique IP** → can **talk directly** to other Pods.
* **CNI plugins** configure **Pod NICs** & provide **isolation**.
* K8s **network services**: **Load Balancing, Service Discovery, Ingress** → enable **external traffic access**.
* Implemented via **K8s objs**: **Services, Ingress, NetworkPolicies**.
* Essential for **scalability, reliability & flexibility** in clusters.
