## What is Kubernetes?

* A **portable and extensible open-source platform** for managing containerized workloads and services.
* Facilitates **declarative configuration** and **automation**.
* **Created by Google**.

## Why use Kubernetes?

* A powerful platform to **deploy and manage containerized applications at scale**.
* Enables **easy scalability**, **application portability**, **high resilience**, and **automation**.
* **Reduces complexity and workload** for operations teams.

## Key Concepts and Architecture

* **Cluster Architecture**: The fundamental architectural concepts behind Kubernetes.
* **Container**: Technology that **packages an application with its runtime dependencies**.
* **Workloads**: Understanding **Pods**, the smallest deployable compute objects in Kubernetes, and the higher-level abstractions used to run them.
* **Services, Load Balancing, and Networking**: Concepts and resources behind **networking and communication between pods**.
* **Storage**: Ways to provide both **temporary and persistent storage** to pods in your cluster.
* **Configuration**: Mechanisms Kubernetes provides for **configuring pods**.
* **Cluster Administration**: Low-level details relevant to **creating and managing a Kubernetes cluster**.
