#scheduling
# Cue

# Key points
- assign pods to worker nodes based on res availability, label, affinity/anti-affinity; taints and toleration.
- Pods => smallest deployable units in k8s, consisting of 1+container that share the same network namespace.
- scheduler assign pods to node, while label are used for matching
- affinity+anti-affinity rules dictate how pods are scheduled based on their relationship with other pods or nodes.

# Summary
Scheduling is the process to assign pods to worker nodes based on  res availability, label, affinity-anti affinity, taints and toleration.
