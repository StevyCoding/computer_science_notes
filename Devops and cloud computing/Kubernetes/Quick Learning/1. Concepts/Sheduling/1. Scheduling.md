# Key points
- **Scheduling**
	- process of assigning workloads to specific nodes in cluster
	- scheduling decision is based on factors such as res availability, node suitability  and workload priorities
- **Benefits**
	- ensure efficient res usage and avoid overloading nodes
- it take into account factors like geographic location, hardware requirements, and app specific needs
# Summary
Scheduling => process  to assign workload to specific nodes in a cluster. Scheduling  decision depends of res availability, suitability, and workload priorities. it Ensure efficient res usage across the cluster and avoid nodes overloading.
