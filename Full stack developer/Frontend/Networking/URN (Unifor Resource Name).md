A URN is another type of URI that is used to uniquely identify a resource by its name or identifier, regardless of its location. Unlike URLs, which provide information about the resource's location, URNs are designed to be persistent and independent of location changes. They are used to uniquely identify resources without specifying how to locate them. An example of a URN is "urn:isbn:0451450523," which uniquely identifies a book by its International Standard Book Number (ISBN).
