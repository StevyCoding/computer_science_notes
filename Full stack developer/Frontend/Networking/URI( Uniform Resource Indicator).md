A URI, or Uniform Resource Identifier, is a string of characters used to identify a resource on the internet. It serves as a compact representation of a resource's location or name. A URI can be used to locate and retrieve the resource using a specific protocol, such as HTTP, FTP, or others. URIs are used extensively in web-related technologies to uniquely identify resources, such as web pages, images, videos, and other online content.
